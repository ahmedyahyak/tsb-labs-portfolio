Brand toolkit · Reference v1

One identity, applied the same way every time.

This page is the single source of truth for how TSB Labs looks, sounds and writes. It covers the logo system, color, typography, graphic language and voice, with downloadable assets at the bottom. When in doubt, copy what is here.

01 / Logo system

Monogram tile plus wordmark.

The identity is typographic: a blue tile carrying TSB in mono, next to the TSB LABS wordmark with the SYSTEMS FOR THE AI ERA descriptor. There is no pictorial logo, and none should be invented.

PRIMARY LOCKUP · ON INKSVG
LOCKUP · ON WARM PAPERSVG
TSB
MONOGRAM · APP ICON / AVATARSVG
TSB
MONOGRAM ON LIGHT · UNCHANGEDSVG

Rules

Clearspace around the lockup is half the tile height on every side.
Minimum sizes: tile 24px digital, lockup 140px wide. Below that, use the monogram alone.
The tile is always Blueprint Blue with warm paper type. Never recolor, outline or add effects.
On photography, place the lockup on an ink panel first. Never directly on a busy image.
The descriptor line SYSTEMS FOR THE AI ERA is always mono, uppercase, letterspaced.
02 / Color

Blueprint blue on ink.

Ink dominates. Blue is the working accent, roughly one part in ten. Warm paper is for light documents and for type sitting on blue. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Rules

Never mix this palette with DevMate red or AI Founder Hub volt. Three brands, three systems.
Blue on ink or paper on blue. Never blue type on the blue tile.
Body text on ink is Body Gray, headings are Warm Paper. Pure white is not used.
03 / Typography

Inter speaks, mono labels.

Two families only. Inter carries headlines and body. JetBrains Mono carries labels, codes, numbers and anything that should feel like part of the machine.

INTER · 400 TO 800
We build the systems that run the business.
Headings 700 to 800, tight letterspacing, minus two percent.
Body 400 at 15 to 17px with relaxed line height around 1.7. Emphasis is 600 in Blue Tint, never italic.
JETBRAINS MONO · 400 TO 700
TSB-2026-024 · OMR 12,500 · STAGE 03 OF 05
Kickers and labels · uppercase · letterspaced .14em to .22em

Rules

Mono is for short strings only: labels, reference codes, prices, statuses. Never paragraphs.
Every section opens with a mono kicker in Bright Blue before the Inter headline.
Numbers in tables and pricing always set in mono so they align.
04 / Graphic language

The blueprint grid.

The background system that makes TSB Labs recognizable at a glance: a faint engineering grid on ink, structured cards, and mono wayfinding.

GRID

Major lines every 96px, minor every 24px, in Blueprint Blue at 5 and 2.5 percent opacity. Always behind content, never competing with it.

KICKERS

Sections are numbered in mono with a short rule line before the text.

05 / Voice and writing

Calm, structured, no hype.

TSB Labs writes like an engineer who respects your time. The company speaks as we. Ahmed is named as founder and as the person personally delivering, which is a selling point, not a footnote.

The line

We build it. You run it.

Rules

Never use em dashes or en dashes. Commas, colons, periods and plain hyphens only.
Short sentences. One idea each. Cut every adjective that does not earn its place.
No hype words: revolutionary, cutting edge, world class, unleash, supercharge.
Honesty is the selling mechanism. State limits plainly, including what we advise clients not to buy.
Confidential clients are described, never named. References offered privately instead.
Prices, dates and stage numbers are stated exactly, in mono, or not at all.
