Services

Four things,
done properly.

We take on a small number of engagements each year so every one gets a builder in the room rather than a project manager relaying messages.

What each one actually covers.

Platforms and apps

Customer apps, field and operations apps, admin dashboards and billing. Built for the people who operate them every day.

AI systems

Assistants and agents wired into real operations: support, sales, back office. AI that does work, not AI that does demos.

Secure foundations

Encrypted data rooms, access control, audit logs, tested backups and disaster recovery. Security is designed in from day one, because it cannot be retrofitted.

Technology leadership

A builder in the room: architecture, vendor selection, security programmes, hiring and running engineers, and honest advice on what not to build.

In practice

What each of these actually means.

Every line below is something we have built and shipped, not a capability list. Where an engagement is under confidentiality we describe the work rather than name the client.

01 · PLATFORMS AND APPS

The software your business runs on

Customer facing apps, field and operations tools, admin consoles, and the billing underneath them. We have built a universal gym membership platform with a member app, a check in portal, an admin console and billing, and a bilingual English and Arabic manufacturing site end to end.

Typically: web app, mobile app, admin console, payments, reporting.

02 · AI SYSTEMS

AI that does work, not demos

Assistants and agents wired into real operations with a human approval gate on anything irreversible. Madar OS is our own: a memory graph of the business, specialist departments, and a permission ring that stops every send, post and payment until a person says yes. We run our own companies on it daily.

Typically: context encoding, connector wiring, approval design, evaluation before trust.

03 · SECURE FOUNDATIONS

Boring infrastructure, deliberately

Encrypted data rooms, access control, audit logs, backups that have been restored rather than merely scheduled. A backup nobody has restored is a hope, not a backup, so we prove it before we call it done.

Typically: access model, audit trail, backup and restore drill, disaster recovery plan.

04 · TECHNOLOGY LEADERSHIP

A builder in the room

Architecture decisions, vendor selection, security programmes, and running engineering when you need someone accountable rather than another agency. We act as technology advisor and build lead to a platform business in Oman, leading development across mobile apps, an operations console and secure infrastructure.

Typically: monthly advisory, architecture review, vendor and hiring decisions, escalation cover.

What we do not do

Said plainly, so nobody wastes a call.

Staff augmentation

We are not a body shop. We take responsibility for an outcome, not for filling a seat on your org chart.

Hourly billing

One agreed number, split into stages. An open meter rewards the wrong things and neither of us should want it.

Work we cannot stand behind

If the timeline or the budget only works by cutting the parts that make it safe, we will say so and decline rather than ship it.

Start a conversation

A builder in the room, not a proposal in the post.

Tell us what is broken or what you need built. If we are not the right fit we will say so in the first call rather than after a month of scoping.
