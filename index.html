02 / What we build

Four things, done properly.

Platforms and apps

Customer apps, field and operations apps, admin dashboards and billing. Built for the people who operate them every day.

AI systems

Assistants and agents wired into real operations: support, sales, back office. AI that does work, not AI that does demos.

Secure foundations

Encrypted data rooms, access control, audit logs, tested backups and disaster recovery. Security is designed in from day one, because it cannot be retrofitted.

Technology leadership

A builder in the room: architecture, vendor selection, security programmes, hiring and running engineers, and honest advice on what not to build.

03 / How we work

Calm, structured, accountable.

01

One fixed price, staged payments

The build is one agreed number, split into stages, so it never surprises you. No hourly billing, no open meters.

02

Acceptance by demonstration

A stage is done when you watch it work: a customer books, a job completes, a payment lands. Not when a document says so.

03

Plain language agreements

Three pages you can read without a lawyer. The protections are all there, one line each.

04

The founder delivers

You deal directly with the person responsible for the build, from first call to handover. A small senior team, AI assisted delivery, no layers.

AFTER DELIVERY

Run it yourself, or have us run it fully from day one

Every build is handed over ready to operate. If you want it fully handled, we run the system on a monthly retainer: hosting, security, monitoring, support and continuous development, billed as one predictable monthly number. Many of our engagements continue this way after delivery.
