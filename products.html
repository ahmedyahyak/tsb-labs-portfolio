PERSONAL / 02STAGE 3 · RUNS ON ITS OWN SERVER

Madar OS, our AI operating system

Our assistant grew into an operating system, a TSB Labs product. One brain with a persistent memory graph of the business, a bench of forty five specialist roles, and runtime search over a library of 89,000 skills it looks up instead of installs. Since September it runs on its own server rather than a laptop: close the lid and it still answers on the phone, books and cancels meetings through the calendar, and copies its database to object storage every night, a copy we prove by restoring it. Hosting costs nothing. It runs its own CRM, sales and follow up lanes as self healing supervised services, every sensitive action passes a permission ring, and we run TSB Labs on it daily, which is how we know what we sell actually works.
