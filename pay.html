02 / Request details and an invoice

Bank transfer is available for any amount and is usually the cheapest route for larger invoices. We do not publish our account details on a public page, because published details get scraped and reused in invoice fraud against the people who trust us.

03 / Before you pay

  • Check the reference. Every TSB Labs invoice carries a code in the form TSB-INV-YYYY-NNN. If a payment request reaches you without one, or the account details differ from what we sent you directly, stop and call before paying.
  • For most invoices, bank transfer is the simpler route. Paying in cryptocurrency means the processor verifies your identity first, which is worth it only if you already hold the coin.
  • We never ask for payment details by email or over a call, and we will never ask you to change bank details by message alone.
  • Crypto checkout is hosted by NOWPayments. The transaction happens on their secure page and never touches ours.
